================================================================================
0. THE MECHANISM, AND ITS EXACTNESS PROOF (stated in the code,
   `ClassScanMemo`, src/genome_inference/realign_score.rs)
================================================================================

The class scan's per-(class-pair, unit) work is a PURE FUNCTION of the
pair's evidence-row bit patterns: the committed accumulation
`total += count * mix_logsumexp(ll_a[u], ll_b[u])` reads, at every
unit, the two rows' column values and nothing else. Two folds whose
ll columns are bit-equal (the merge's own `ll_bits_equal` predicate,
whichever encoding each row took) therefore produce BIT-IDENTICAL
class pair sums against every third fold, and their hom pairs are
identical too. The scan computes ONE sum per unordered (row-class,
row-class) pair and shares it with every bit-equal pair.

EXACTNESS BY CONSTRUCTION: for folds f, g with representatives
r = canon[f], s = canon[g], class_pair_sum(r, s) executes the
IDENTICAL f64 op sequence as class_pair_sum(f, g) — at each unit the
cursor reads the same bits (column equality is exactly the
`ll_bits_equal` predicate), `count` is per-unit, the accumulation
walks units ascending in both — and IEEE-754 round-to-nearest is a
deterministic function of the input bits and the op sequence, so the
results are equal bit for bit. The memo SHARES VALUES, it never
reorders: every pair's own flat slot is filled with the
representative pair's value, so class_lls, best, the called set, the
QUAL terms and every receipt field are the direct scan's own bits.

THE KEY DISCIPLINE (the fold-signature doctrine): bucketing by the
canonical column-content hash (`column_key_hash` — the row's pinned
entries whose ll bits differ from the elsewhere constant's, as
(unit, bits) pairs in unit order, the entry count fed last;
encoding-independent by construction, O(pinned) per row, never the
full O(units) column walk), with every bucket candidate decided by
the EXACT predicate `ll_bits_equal` on match — a hash collision can
never merge. The representative is the class's smallest fold index
(deterministic).

SITES (all the scan's exact-sum consumers, one memo table per
locus, built once on the FINAL post-merge matrix): the exhaustive
scan (the assessment form's own path — phase A computes each
distinct canonical pair sum once, in parallel, phase B fills every
pair's own flat slot), the bounded scan's stage-one rounds (the
round's distinct canonical sums, persistent across rounds), stage
two (the truth pair + the kept set), and the best-outside fallback's
walk and tied sweep. The legacy sparse-skip/compare arms keep the
direct form (measurement forms, off at the defaults).

THE TOGGLE: IMPG_CLASS_SCAN_MEMO=off restores the direct per-pair
form verbatim (the bounded scan's own toggle doctrine); the default
is ON. No output label is warranted — the memo changes no emitted
byte either way (the gates below prove it), and the phase census
(env IMPG_LOCUS_PHASE_CENSUS) receipts the hit rates per shape.

THE UNIT TESTS (cargo test --release --lib 586/0, 5 ignored — 584
committed + the two new):
- `memoized_scan_is_bit_identical_to_direct_on_randomized_rows` —
  the randomized bit-row oracle: 60 cases, each with exact clones,
  cross-encoding twins (extra pinned entries at exactly E —
  invisible to the column), and one-ulp NEAR-TWIN negative controls;
  per case: the canonical classes must be EXACTLY the brute-force
  `ll_bits_equal` classes (the exact predicate decides, never the
  hash), and the memoized two-phase scan (the scan site's own shape)
  must reproduce the direct per-pair accumulation BIT FOR BIT at
  every pair.
- `scan_memo_canonicalizes_only_bit_equal_columns` — the key
  discipline: pinned-at-E entries are invisible (folds differing
  only in them share one class), a one-ulp non-E difference is its
  own class, the representative is the class's min, and the shared
  values are bit-identical (incl. the all-E class's hom pair).
- the bounded stage-one determinism test now also runs the MEMOIZED
  stage one and gates its kept values against the direct form's own
  bits (bit-for-bit, plus the best).

5. THE HONEST VERDICT ON THE 37.7% FLOOR (the probe's tranche-1
   band vs the kept corpus's measured truth)
================================================================================

The 23.9-37.7% band was measured by the frontier-1 probe at the
TRANCHE-1 monsters (T03226 l0/l10, T00940 l0/l1/l5, T03901 l10/l13)
— the fold-saturated segdup plateaus whose bit-equal rows the merge
REFUSES wholesale (outside-bubble: the segdup copies are not
alignment-connected to the axis bubble). Those substrates were
REAPED with the disk crisis (tranche-1's definitional smalls are
gone — the ledger's own receipt), and tranche 2 drew NO S_SEG
members (its strata are S_EXP/S_BIND/S_MAR), so the T00940-class
plateau is NOT re-derivable from the kept corpus today.

What the kept corpus measures instead (the receipts above):
- at the COMMITTED stack the memo's catchment IS the merge's
  refusal set — L1: 1,022 pairs at one locus of 19 (3.1%), ZERO
  elsewhere; T09724: 0-3.5% per locus, 0.5% at the fold-heavy
  monster — because the second reduction (the merge) already
  collapses every bit-equal row it admits, and its refusals are few
  at these shapes;
- at the PRE-MERGE anatomy form (the sanctioned one-env form, the
  l15-class shape's own territory) the memo's live band is
  7.7-41.8% per locus — the probe band's lower edge landed EXACTLY
  (23.9% at l0) and its upper edge EXCEEDED (41.8% at l6) — but the
  wall-weighted saving is small because the fold-heavy l9 (the
  437s term) carries only 7 duplicate rows (3.1%).

The fourth reduction is therefore IMPLEMENTED, PROVEN EXACT
(byte-identical at every gate, both forms, both targets), and
essentially FREE (the build is O(pinned) — 0.42s at the 246k-unit
monster; the lookup O(1)); its heavy catchment (the plateau class's
37.7%-band) lives in the reaped tranche-1 stratum and CANNOT be
re-measured at a real plateau until a plateau substrate exists
again (the panel precompute's era will carry re-derivable
substrates). The tail term at the kept corpus stands as measured:
NOT collapsed by the memo — the plateau's collapse is bounded by
the memo's construction catchment (the merge's refusals), which the
tranche-1 probe measured at 23.9-37.7% and which the pre-merge form
re-measures live at 7.7-41.8% per locus here.

## 1. THE SYSTEM (one paragraph)

A diploid genome is inferred against the HPRC panel **without realigning
reads to a reference**: the sample's reads are projected ONCE onto the
pangenome syng graph (the FM index of syncmer walks; the syng node ids are
the coordinates), then per territory a haplotype-aware realignment-likelihood
model calls a diplotype at every locus (the alignment-induced locality
domains, the territory-image extents, the model-internal QUAL cluster
machinery). The product emits, per locus: the called pair, the class
identity, the QUAL — and now **the reconstructed diploid sequence itself**
(hap1/hap2 + the tied classes' coalesced called set, `--emit-sequences`,
proven on all five localities: every call → a sequence, identity-checked
against the official_allwave machinery's alignments).

## 7. THE HONEST CAVEATS

- **The hotbed failure mode, named to the locality:** the chr1 pilot
  (the segdup block) is the weak panel at every depth (4/22 rank-1) and
  pays the masking price (median 32.1, 13.6% <QV20). Where the target's
  material is not shared with the panel, neither the with-HG002 call nor
  the stranger reconstruction can recover it.
- **Frame sensitivity:** the window-grain sweep measured the verdict to be
  grain-dependent where coarse windows swallow repeat material (L2-deep
  87.7%→59.8% at 20kb; L1-35x 47.8%→19.9%) — the committed 10KB grain is
  the measured recommendation; per-bp invariance is a locality property,
  not a law. The official-QV convention itself is convention-sensitive
  (the aligned-span effect, the non-injective per-hap-best, the indel
  model) — the near-twin band restatement quantifies it; no campaign
  verdict changed.
- **What is measured vs extrapolated:** MEASURED — every per-territory
  step's wall, the five-locality chains, the projection (both
  configurations, identity-gated), the 12-lane aggregate point, the masked
  legs, the yeast sweep. EXTRAPOLATED (arithmetic at measured lane counts
  and RSS, stated as such) — the genome-wide wave (~12,400 territories at
  the measured per-lane peak), the per-territory ~23s at 35x (arithmetic
  from the 300x phase table at the ~6× smaller read mass), the panel-side
  precompute 256-wide, and the overlapped ~21min bound (the streaming
  restructure's, NOT implemented, owner-gated).
- **Truth discipline:** all called-vs-truth numbers live assessment-side
  only (the `--truth-qv` test mode; the truth haplotypes are an INPUT);
  the product output — calls and the sequence emission — is truth-free.
  The human localities are HG002 (a panel member) and the masked legs are
  its held-out twin; the yeast fleet is a closed loop on error-free
  simulated reads — neither is a wild-sample claim.
- **Co-tenant noise:** ±20% is stated on every throughput rate (the box
  is shared); walls are single-run receipts, not medians.

## Per-record likelihood comparison (owner go-ahead 2026-10-02): the COSIGT-style comparison at read granularity, in the graph's coordinates

**The go-ahead.** The condensed node+edge coordinates STAY (shared material stored once; the graph remains the coordinate system); what changes is the COMPARISON: from the mass-cosine to per-record likelihoods. Two measured blind spots must be fixed: (1) records landing on differing material must vote differently under different candidates — divergence becomes multiplicative evidence; the 90%-mass divergent rivals that scored 78.8–99.99% of the maximum cosine must now separate; (2) predicted-but-unobserved mass must be penalized — a candidate using material where no records land loses probability (the zero-count structure).

**The model (derived; no thresholds, no tuning constants).** Fix a locus. The observation universe U is the union of all candidate rows' node+edge usage (the established fixed convention; mass outside is dropped). Every universe key g carries an observed count C_g: the sum of the routed shares m_r of the records whose merged placement spans cover it — the SAME observed side the cosines aggregate, and the per-record granularity lives exactly here: each record's share mass restricted to the placements consistent with a candidate is its addends m_r at the candidate's keys. A diplotype class c (an unordered pair of coalesced graph rows) predicts a rate per key:

```
lambda_c(g) = M * E_c(g) / Q_c   on keys(c),      Q_c = sum_{g in keys(c)} E_c(g)
```

E_c(g) is the class's summed READ-COVERING INCIDENCE over its spelled instances of g: the number of read-start positions whose READ_LENGTH window contains the key's window ([bp, bp+k) for a node; [bp_left, bp_right+k) for an adjacency), clipped to the path — the structured-poisson machinery's exposure conversion (a feature expects share mass proportional to the number of read placements that can contain it), applied per graph key. The budget M is the locus's realized total share mass: the empirical-Bayes calibration of the per-copy depth-15 expectation to the realized routed-share scale. THE CALIBRATION IS FORCED BY MEASUREMENT, not chosen: the depth form depth*q/(L−k+1 window incidence) predicts a uniform ~8.8 share units per single-copy key, while the realized universe densities at these loci span ~0.05–4.0 (measured from the stage-1 receipts: e.g. chrMT locus2 mass 1,241.9 over 6,060 universe keys; chrI locus16 39,164.2 over 16,441) — a raw depth-anchored rate is systematically over-predicted at every locus by a locus-dependent factor, and any raw-Poisson ranking under it collapses toward minimal predicted material. The realized budget M is the only per-locus, candidate-independent, constant-free anchor: under the hypothesis that c is the true diplotype, c's material generated the locus's routed share mass, distributed over its keys by exposure-weighted repeat multiplicity. Keys the candidate does not spell are explained by the max-entropy null — the uniform background rate beta = M/|U| over the fixed observation universe (the omission-charge structure of the B1/B2 arcs: observed-but-unpredicted features are charged at a pure background rate).

The class's negative log-likelihood is the per-key Poisson count structure over the whole universe:

```
NLL_c = sum_{g in keys(c)} [lambda_c(g) - C_g ln lambda_c(g) + lnG(C_g+1)]
      + sum_{g outside keys(c)} [beta        - C_g ln beta        + lnG(C_g+1)]
```

Both blind spots are structural: a candidate key with NO records (C_g = 0) is charged its full zero-count rate lambda_c(g) — predicted-but-unobserved material loses probability, linearly in the predicted mass; and records landing on material a candidate does not spell leave their share mass at the background rate, where the explaining candidate earns C_g*ln lambda_c(g) — each record's restricted share mass enters the per-key counts, so records landing on differing material vote differently and their evidence multiplies through the Poisson structure. The log-factorial count-costs lnG(C_g+1) are candidate-independent (every universe key is charged exactly once, explained or not) and cancel from ranking; they are carried in the emitted log-likelihood for honesty, summed in sorted key order so the absolute values are run-stable.

**The exact per-record decomposition (requirement b).** The total is the sum over records of their per-record log-likelihoods — the identity

```
nll_{r,c} = M/R + (|U|-K_c)*beta/R
            - sum_{g in K_r ∩ keys(c)} m_r*ln lambda_c(g)
            - sum_{g in K_r \ keys(c)} m_r*ln beta
            + sum_{g in K_r} (m_r/C_g)*lnG(C_g+1)
```

with R the locus's contributing record count and K_r the record's universe-restricted covered key set: the rate budgets thin over the R records, each record's share-mass count terms enter through its own covered keys (consistent at the class's rate, inconsistent at the background), and the count-costs split by the record's share of each key's count. Summing over records reconstitutes the class NLL exactly (unit-proven in `likelihood_per_record_decomposition_sums_to_class_nll`). The emitted per-record sidecar carries this term for the named classes (the winner, the truth class, the best divergent rival); the exhaustive walk itself computes the equal totals incrementally per pair (below).

**The incremental/memoized walk.** The closed form collapses the NLL to per-class quantities computable in ONE O(keys) merge per arm per pair — the same shape as the cosine pair walk: NLL_c = CONST + M_c*ln(Q_c*beta/M) - A_c - K_c*beta, with A_c = sum_{g in keys(c)} C_g*ln E_c(g), M_c the covered mass, K_c the distinct key count, Q_c = Q_i + Q_j additive over the two rows (the merged usage's incidences add), and CONST = M + Gamma_U + |U|*beta - M*ln beta a per-locus constant. FAIL-CLOSED: a massless locus (M = 0) emits null for every class; a class with observed mass on a zero-incidence key (no read placement can cover it, so the candidate's rate there is zero) or with zero total incidence and covered mass emits null — ineligible, the zero-norm guard's likelihood analogue. Both arms (nodes-only and nodes+edges) are computed in the same walk; the product arm is the combined one, the established convention of every QUAL stage.

**Ranking and QUAL.** The exhaustive full-domain ranking is by log-likelihood over every unordered pair of coalesced graph rows (exact IEEE comparison; no epsilon enters the likelihood era's ranking or tie call — the stage-1 1e-12 window was a cosine-scale convention and does not transfer). QUAL = -10*log10(1 - p); unbounded (p = 1: k = 1, no divergent rival) and massless loci emit null, never a clamp. The full-domain flat-prior posterior is emitted beside the product form as the natural measurement (`qual_posterior_logsumexp`, `qual_posterior_top_cluster`).

## QUAL emission, cluster form: the similarity delta BETWEEN CLUSTERS (owner ruling, 2026-10-01; corrects the delta form's alternative)

**The ruling.** The delta form measured every non-called class as an alternative, so a domain of near-identical runner-ups kept Q ≈ 3 everywhere: "there's 10 sequences that are almost identical and we pick one of them; they shouldn't get penalized as if they're not." The near-twin runner-ups are not real alternatives; the quality must measure the separation from the NEXT ACTUALLY DIVERGENT cluster of sequences, not from the ninth twin. Picking any member of a near-identical band is a fine sequence call.

**Material distance.** For two candidate classes (unordered pairs of coalesced graph rows; the class signature is the merged node+edge usage multiset of its two rows), the distance is the OBSERVED READ MASS carried on the material where their node+edge usage signatures differ — the sum, over the symmetric difference of the two usage multisets weighted by the multiplicity difference, of the observed routed share sitting on each differing segment/adjacency (the same evidence units the cosines use; exactly the quantity the stage-2 tie anatomy already measured as zero for bit-exact ties). A second view is reported beside it: the signature-cosine distance, 1 − cosine of the RAW usage multisets (multiplicity vectors; the per-copy depth is a common positive factor and cancels). Both derive from the existing receipts' machinery — no new runs, no new evidence channels.

**The cluster cut (derived knee rule, no tuning constants).** From the winner (the first class of the bit-identical called set), compute the material distance to every other candidate class and sort. The near-identical band appears as a dense run of small distances followed by the divergent tail, so the band-to-tail transition is the MAXIMAL RELATIVE JUMP r_i = (d_{i+1} − d_i)/d_{i+1} ∈ [0,1] between consecutive distances (0/0 := 0); ties resolve to the LARGEST index so the band absorbs the full dense run, and the cut distance is d_{i*} — the last band point. The called cluster is every class within the cut. A spectrum whose maximal relative jump does not exceed the MEDIAN relative jump is scale-free (exact geometric growth: every jump equal) and HAS NO KNEE — a measurement, not an error: the cut then stays at 0 and the nearest strictly-positive distance is reported as the fallback ("the distance to the nearest candidate that carries observed mass on differing material"; substantial-ness is read off the reported number, never thresholded). The exactly-zero distances (bit-identical material, or differing material carrying no observed mass) are the innermost band, always inside the cluster — the knee is derived over the strictly-positive distances only, because a jump out of an exact zero is the maximal relative jump r = 1 by definition and would otherwise pin the cut at the zero boundary and split the band. With fewer than two positive distances no jump can dominate the median and no knee is claimed. The full sorted distance spectrum (with aligned similarities and signature-cosine distances) is emitted per locus, so where the cut lands is auditable evidence, not an assertion.

**Cluster-level QUAL.** The delta form's formula is UNCHANGED but applied BETWEEN CLUSTERS: k = the number of DIVERGENT clusters (not members) whose best candidates bit-tie the maximum — the bit-tied called classes clustered by the same near-identity relation (single-linkage components at the knee cut, chains included; the winner's own cluster counts); a = the best similarity among candidates OUTSIDE the near-identical called cluster(s) — the best genuinely divergent rival; s_win = the winner's similarity; p = s_win/(k·s_win + a); QUAL = −10·log10(1 − p). Members of the called cluster(s) never supply a, never count in k, never lower QUAL. Derivation: the hypotheses are the k divergent tied-maximum clusters (each carrying s_win) and the best divergent rival (carrying a); forced two-way normalization k·s_win vs a with uniform truth inside a bit-tied cluster (the measured tie anatomy: every differing node/edge carries zero observed read mass) gives the confidence in the single emitted material draw p = (k·s_win/(k·s_win + a))·(1/k) = s_win/(k·s_win + a) — the delta form's algebra with clusters as the units. Bit-identical coalescing stays innermost (distance 0, always in the cluster).

**Reduction checks.** (i) An all-near-identical domain with a far divergent tail rates HIGH: the knee absorbs the band, the tail supplies a ≪ s_win (the owner's ten-sequences case, unit-tested in `cluster_form_qual_owner_reductions`). (ii) A bit-tie of DIVERGENT materials keeps the honest low bound: no knee lands between them, so k = 2 and p ≤ 1/2 — real ambiguity kept. (iii) A divergent near-score rival sits outside the cluster, supplies a ≈ s_win, and Q stays low. (iv) Bit-identical members are invisible: distance 0 is innermost, never supplies a, never counts in k. Monotonicity in a and k holds at cluster level by the same algebra (unit-proven; checker-asserted on the measured spectra, including the counterfactual that absorbing the best divergent rival into the band raises p).

## Correction to the first checkpoint's tie claim (2026-10-01, QUAL stage)

## QUAL emission, delta form: the similarity DELTA (owner ruling, 2026-10-01; supersedes the share form above)

**The share form is MEASURED FALSIFIED.** The forced normalization `share = s_win / Σ s_c` over every distinct class tracked the called set's fraction of the domain's TOTAL similarity mass, not the similarity delta: in these exhaustive domains thousands of eligible near-twin classes each carry substantial similarity, so every measured share lay between 4e-5 and 1e-2 and every finite QUAL between 0.0002 and 0.37 — no discrimination between a confident unique maximum and a genuine tie (the k=2 tie loci scored 0.0006/0.0003, indistinguishable from the separated unique maxima). **Derivation of the delta form.** Keep every piece of stage-2 machinery — material-class coalescing (bit-identical node+edge usage signatures merge; class weight = the class's OWN similarity value), called set = distinct classes at the bit-identical maximum, null emission for unbounded/massless loci, the tie-evidence stream — and replace ONLY the quality formula. The called set's confidence over its best alternative is the FORCED TWO-WAY NORMALIZATION `k*s_win` vs `a`: class mass under exclusive hypotheses (either the truth is in the called set or the best alternative wins; classes below `a` are strictly dominated and enter nowhere — any wider normalization would reintroduce the falsified domain-mass dependence). Within a bit-tied called set the truth is UNIFORM — measured in the tie anatomy: every differing node/edge carries zero observed read mass, so this read sample cannot distinguish the tied classes. The emitted call is a single material draw, so the confidence in it is

```
p = (k*s_win / (k*s_win + a)) * (1/k) = s_win / (k*s_win + a)
QUAL = -10*log10(1 - p)
```

with `k` = distinct material classes bit-tied at the maximum `s_win`, and `a` = the best similarity among ALL classes outside the called set (`a = 0` if none). NO free parameters: the Phred convention and the forced two-way normalization are the only derived forms, both documented here.

## The read-matched observed convention (owner-approved option b, 2026-11-05): the likegt fix

The SMEAR's cause is the haploid-era **placement-span** credit: a routed record's observation was every panel key CONTAINED in its chained placement span — the extent from its first anchor's start to its last anchor's end. A MEM chain only requires its conserved-flank anchors to appear at the read's offsets on SOME panel path (the GBWT match advances node-by-offset and never compares the intervening bases), so a chain that bridges another strain's variant region credits the record's share on the variant sequence — material the read does NOT match. The remedy stage measured this at 21-34% of locus mass.

**The convention, stated as a rule in graph coordinates (no thresholds, no tuning constants):**

1. **OBSERVED MASS.** A record's share mass credits ONLY the material it actually matched: its anchors' own k-mer spans (the exact matches the MEM extraction verified), at each verified occurrence, in the occurrence's path coordinates. The matched material of one occurrence is the union of its anchor windows `[start+rel, start+rel+k)`; overlapping and exactly-abutting windows merge into one interval (contiguous matched material). A positive gap between windows splits the interval, and **unmatched gaps contribute nothing** — no span-containment credit across flanks, no chaining across variant regions. Partial overlaps credit by the established containment convention on both sides: a panel key (node or edge) is credited iff its full k-mer window lies inside ONE occurrence's matched interval.
2. **EDGE VOTES.** A record votes for an edge traversal (the adjacency of two consecutive panel steps) only where it genuinely spans the junction with matched material on BOTH abutting sides: both abutting steps' windows must lie inside the SAME occurrence's matched interval. A chain that bridged a variant region no longer votes — its two flank intervals are separate. Placements of one record never merge with each other, even when they abut end-to-end on the same path: no single placement spans their seam, so no edge vote forms there (node credit is per-placement containment, deduplicated per record as before — the record-once convention is unchanged).

**Implementation (assessment-side only, env `IMPG_COSINE_READ_MATCHED`; production runs never set it):** `build_in_window_obs` emits, beside the unchanged placement spans, each record's per-occurrence merged matched intervals (`RecordMatchedSpans`); `GraphLocus::build` (`record_observation_intervals`) consumes them for the observed masses and edge votes of the graph-space instrument (the likelihood stage's record-once routed-share masses per universe key). The expected side (candidate rows' spelled material, exposures, incidences), the candidate domain, the ranking and cluster-QUAL machinery are untouched. With the gate unset the placement-span convention is emitted and consumed exactly as before (bit-identical with the committed receipts). The S2 instance-level exoneration and every product consumer keep the placement spans.

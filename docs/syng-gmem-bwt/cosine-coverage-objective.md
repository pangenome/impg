# Diploid local coverage-vector comparison — owner-review design

Status: **design only; not implemented for selection** (2026-10-01). This supersedes Item 3 *as a proposal for a new likelihood*. It does not erase the independent need to represent missing SK1 truth material or prove two physical routes. No production path, output score, reference column, or PR is changed by this document.

## 1. Replace the local comparison form

The existing local class-pair sweep enumerates diplotypes, but ranks them by the background-relative Poisson/window-mass objective and its in-window omission charge. It is **not** the COSIGT coverage-vector comparison. On the balanced 15×/15× saved sample, its selected exact truth pair is 4/502 where both truth rows exist. The read-only diagnostic `examples/panel_route_spine/cosine_probe.rs` measured two alternative cosines against the *same* truth, selected, eight top unrestricted old-loss pairs and eight top port-viable old-loss pairs (9–18 distinct physical pairs/locus). On the 498 wrong-selected windows, the physical placement-coverage cosine ranks truth first, including ties, **447/498** and at most second **477/498**; the old pooled-window FeatureKey mass under cosine itself yields **422/498** and **461/498**. This supports replacing the **comparison**, while disproving a claim that the pooled vector alone is sufficient to explain all wrong calls. These are restricted ranks, **not an exhaustive genotype accuracy claim**.

Use one fixed, local material coordinate universe for *every* candidate at a locus: panel path and disjoint base-position intervals in the union of its physically spelled candidate intervals. The observed vector `x` projects each saved routed read-MEM record onto its actual physical placements in this universe. Its existing equal routing share is `record multiplicity / number of touched partitions`; deduplicate overlapping subwalk spans **within a record** before adding its share at a base. A multiply placed record can support multiple panel paths according to that same marginal share; this is not a uniquely aligned read. The expected vector of a physical row is depth 15 on every base it spells, zero elsewhere. An unordered diplotype `i,j` expects `e_i+e_j`; score

```
cos(x, e_i+e_j) = (<x,e_i>+<x,e_j>) /
                 (||x|| sqrt(||e_i||²+||e_j||²+2<e_i,e_j>)).
```

No omission-charge term, multiplicity background, pooled Poisson loss, or guessed SK1 truth enters this selection score. Include predicted-only bases in the expected norm: gratuitously covered material **reduces** cosine. Preserve zeros and duplicated-copy dosage; reject/mark a zero-norm observation or candidate rather than silently awarding a score. Keep canonical source intervals, orientations, and distinct physical row IDs even when vectors tie. Compare QV/tie semantics only after measuring exact and near-ties; no ad hoc cosine threshold or probability calibration is assumed. Assessment truth remains inaccessible to inference.

The expensive observed projection and per-row statistics are computed **once per locus**. Each row needs `d_i=<x,e_i>` and `n_i=||e_i||²`; `g_ij=<e_i,e_j>` is the bp-weighted overlap of two source-path interval sets (or a sparse-vector dot product). Then every pair score requires only O(1) arithmetic. Full `R(R+1)/2` unordered pairs cost O(R²) arithmetic, without enumerating features again per pair. Stream scores/rank/ties rather than retaining a dense matrix; share an expected vector only for **exactly identical coverage vectors**. The old Poisson/FeatureKey class equivalence is NOT evidence that two rows have identical physical coverage, so it cannot silently merge their new scores. Preserve named reference scores, once-per-material accounting and junction census as observational products; their old objective values cannot be interchanged with cosine numbers.

## 2. Repair local admission separately; do not hide the third defect

The saved v2 product says the exact truth pair is in the candidate domain at 502 loci, but `spine_viability` admits both truth rows at only **5/502**. S288C truth is viable 502/502; the SK1 truth row is viable 5/502. `examples/panel_route_spine/mod.rs::spine_viability` intersects whole-component forward and backward reachability through `successors_ref` built from `genome::port_word_seams`, with `spans_feasible_local`. From the saved `truth_allele_link_status`, 48 of the 497 rejected SK1 rows lack a direct incoming and/or outgoing link; **449 have both immediate links but cannot reach both ends through the port-word graph**. The attested truth-piece intervals have no within-row overlapping same-source segments at any of these 502 loci; span feasibility is not the measured blocker. This is a global port-grid reachability constraint being misused as the *local genotype candidate* admission rule, not a failure of cosine to rank the allele.

**Proposed, separately owner-gated admission semantics:** score every locally valid, real physical row in the candidate domain even if the old whole-component port-word graph cannot connect it. Local validity still checks nonempty, properly oriented source intervals, internal nonoverlap, source bounds and junction representation; do not admit an empty physical allele or manufacture an SK1 correspondence. Keep actual physical route legality in a later chain/route layer: test same-source continuity/gap-filled attested seams and novel junctions through the established correlation-phasing evidence, source-span capacity, endpoint legality, and final two-route reconstruction/rescore. Distinguish *local genotype likelihood* from *whole-chromosome path reachability*. Before changing any admission code, trace the 48 direct missing links and 449 cascading disconnected rows by source/port/axis boundary, compare the truth routes' legal physical continuations, and obtain owner review of the corrected transition/admission rule. A permissive `viable=true` patch without that proof is forbidden.

The other **805/1,307** loci lack the exact truth pair in the candidate domain in the first place (chrIV and chrXV entirely); cosine and viability cannot recover an absent SK1 row. That is the partition-native/graph-frame admission task, distinct from 497 rejected rows **within** the 502 expressible loci and distinct from the 554 assessment-side sequence-orthology brackets.

## 3. Exhaustive certified pilot before any production selection rewrite

Use the factored row score above to enumerate **all** real diplotype pairs in each of the 502 old-expressible windows, not the restricted 9–18 alternatives in the first diagnostic. Hold one fixed, complete candidate-material coordinate universe per window; verify record-share and interval-union accounting on self/duplicated/reverse rows. Compute the truth's rank, exact tie set and selected-vs-truth margin over (a) every locally valid domain pair, **without the old port-reachability exclusion**, and (b) the old viable subset for a controlled comparison. Report full rank histogram and #1/<=2 count for 502/502, including the 498 former wrong calls and the 5 currently viable truth loci. Name any new higher-cosine competing pair by its source intervals; diagnose discordant read placements before changing thresholds or restricting candidates. Verify sparse interval-row dot products against a brute-force per-base cosine on source-named small loci. Gate the memory/CPU cost with streaming O(R²) arithmetic and the existing 64GiB external-RSS/guard receipts; no complete dense candidate-by-feature matrix is required.

**Promotion gates, in order:** (1) owner reviews this objective/admission separation and approves feature semantics, (2) measured physical route and reference validation accepts the proposed eligibility/transition repair without dropping score or reference columns, (3) the exhaustive cosine rank pilot confirms sufficient full-domain truth ranking and explains misses. Only then may a separately approved implementation replace the *selection* local score; remeasure dosage, exact selected truth source pairs, the owner's injective assessable-distance yardstick and assignment tracks on the unchanged mask, reference columns and independent RSS. The current 447/498 count authorizes a design, **not a shipped objective or a prediction of 447 exact product calls**.
